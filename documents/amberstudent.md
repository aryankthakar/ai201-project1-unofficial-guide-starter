#### Frequently Asked Questions

### Is University View part of the University of Maryland?

No. University View is a privately owned and professionally managed community.

### Can I live with friends, or can you match me with roommates?

You can select your roommate(s), or you can apply on your own and we can match you with other students. Applicants answer a short list of lifestyle questions to help in the matching process.

### What is included in the rate?

Each resident at University View pays a monthly rental installment with a flat rate that includes:

* Furnishings
* Internet
* In-unit laundry
* Community features — fitness center, pool, shuttle, etc.

### What size are the apartments?

The spacious accommodations at University View range from studio (509 square feet) through 2-bedroom (700 to 1,100 square feet) and 4-bedroom (1,125 to 1,425 square feet) with both private bathrooms and walk-in closets available.

### Are the apartments furnished?

Yes. The furniture package at University View includes a full-size bed, dresser, desk and chair in each bedroom, plus a sofa, coffee table, end table, 50″ HDTV and entertainment stand in each living room. The kitchen is also furnished with a dining table and chairs. To see a sample of the furniture and other finishes, please visit our model residences.

### Will my financial aid cover charges at University View?

Your living costs at University View can be covered by financial aid, just like on-campus housing. Any available financial aid funds, after university charges are satisfied, are released to the student for non-institutional expenses, such as off-campus housing. These funds can be used to pay charges at U\View. Normal payment deadlines will continue to apply.

### Is University View open to non-students?

Nearly all the residents at University View are students at the University of Maryland and the per-person contracts are also designed with students in mind. University View is an Equal Opportunity housing provider and welcomes applications from non-students and residents with children, at no extra cost other than regular charges for each bedroom space selected by the resident. In order to match roommates in shared apartments with college students, roommate consent is required for any occupant who is not a college student.

### What kind of safety and security are provided?

University View has a monitored fire sprinkler system, electronic locks and individually locking bedroom doors. Community courtesy patrol officers are on-site as well. Management staff can be reached by a 24-hour phone number.

### What are the policies on pets, smoking and guests?

University View is a no-pets community. The community is smoke-free in all indoor and adjacent areas. Guests are welcome at all times, subject to the consent of roommates and security considerations. Overnight guests are welcome, with limits on the number of days and guests.

### Can I park my vehicle at University View?

University View offers optional paid parking in our attached parking garage or in surface lots right across the street. Limited guest parking is also available on a metered basis.

### What other transportation options will I have?

University View is connected directly to the University of Maryland campus by footbridge, and is a short walk to Engineering, Computer Science and Environmental Science buildings. The community is also served by UMD shuttle buses, seven days a week during the Fall and Spring semesters. Shuttles stop right outside the front door, running to various campus stops every 10 minutes from 7:30am to 5:30pm weekdays, and from 10am to 5pm on weekends. Night shuttle service continues through 9:50pm Sunday through Thursday, and through 2:30am Friday and Saturday nights.

### Can I see a model apartment?

Yes. Please request an appointment for a personal tour, or just stop by to see us and we’ll be happy to show you the community.
