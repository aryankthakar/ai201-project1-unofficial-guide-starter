About University View

Welcome to University View, your ultimate off-campus living experience for UMD students in College Park, MD. With on-site shuttle service and a scenic walking bridge, commuting to campus is a breeze. Whether you're studying in the Social Study area,... or relaxing in the Lounge, we have the perfect options to fit your vibe. Stay active with 24/7 access to our two fitness centers, complete with equipment, a barre/yoga studio, and so much more. Our fully furnished homes are wired for stylish living, featuring dishwashers, flat screen TVs, and private bathrooms for every roommate. Welcome to your new home at University View, just steps away from UMD and the vibrant College Park community.
